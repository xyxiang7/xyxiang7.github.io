I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [School of Engineering and Applied Sciences](https://seas.harvard.edu/), Harvard University, where I am advised by [Minlan Yu](https://minlanyu.seas.harvard.edu/index.html) and [Eddie Kohler](https://www.read.seas.harvard.edu/~kohler/). Prior to Harvard, I got my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on system software, with a particular interest in understanding the principles behind building efficient, reliable, and extensible software systems, especially for distributed settings.
